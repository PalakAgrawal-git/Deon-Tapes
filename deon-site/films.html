Applications & formulations

Seven film grades, one calendering line.

Each grade is compounded and calendered to its end use — all available up to 1700 mm wide.

01

High-Clarity Transparent & Clear Films

Engineered for applications where flawless optical transmission and surface flatness are critical. These flexible sheets provide superior tensile strength and barrier performance.

Primary industrial uses

  • Surface protection — premium protective table covers
  • Office & archival — protective stationery sleeves
  • Facility segregation — heavy-duty industrial curtains
  • Commercial packaging — clear-view protective packaging

Technical parameters: Max width up to 1700 mm; adjustable plasticizer ratios for varying shore hardness (rigid to highly flexible).

02

Pigmented Coloured & Opaque Films

Compounded with dense, high-grade colour masterbatches for bright, UV-stable pigmentation and uniform colour dispersion across the full width of the web. Available in any custom colour.

Primary industrial uses

  • Electrical insulation — high-dielectric tape backings
  • Retail & logistics — general-purpose consumer packaging
  • Safety & marking — industrial lane marking
  • Weather protection — waterproof rainwear

Technical parameters: Fine-tuned formulations up to 1700 mm width to optimise adhesive anchorage and printability for high-speed coating lines.

03

Tactile Embossed & Textured Films

Processed with in-line, temperature-controlled metal embossing rollers immediately after the final calender pass to lock in clean, deep textures that resist flattening.

Primary industrial uses

  • Home & kitchen — non-slip shelf liners
  • Bathroom furnishings — anti-cling shower curtains
  • Consumer goods — cosmetic bags
  • Interior overlays — protective decorative surfaces

Technical parameters: Up to 1700 mm wide in double-sided matte, sand, diamond or custom geometric patterns per application.

04

Heavy-Duty Agricultural & Greenhouse Films

Chemically fortified to endure extreme environmental conditions without yellowing, embrittlement or premature mechanical failure.

Primary industrial uses

  • Agricultural shielding — UV-stabilised crop-protection canopy hoods
  • Agritech enclosures — commercial greenhouse skins
  • Climate management — thermal insulation barriers

Technical parameters: Seamless wide rolls up to 1700 mm, deeply integrated with UV absorbers, thermal stabilisers and anti-dripping additive packages.

05

High-Yield Advertising & Signage Base Media

Developed as flat, receptive base materials for subsequent coating, printing or structural reinforcing scrim lamination layers.

Primary industrial uses

  • Outdoor advertising — substrate for front-lit and backlit banners
  • Display marketing — commercial light-box media
  • Large-format graphics — wide-format graphic films

Technical parameters: Strict thermal dimensional stability across the full 1700 mm width to avoid shrinkage or wrinkling during secondary lamination.

06

High-Elongation Inflatable & Toy-Grade Films

Formulated under strict safety standards for maximum structural flexibility and tear resistance along welded or high-stress seams.

Primary industrial uses

  • Aquatic recreation — high-integrity pool floats
  • Leisure hardware — heavy-duty inflatable pool liners
  • Recreational products — elastic toys and beach balls

Technical parameters: Wide-web manufacturing with non-toxic, phthalate-free compliance aligned to international children's safety acts.

07

Industrial Substrate & Multi-Layer Lamination Layers

Engineered as foundational core webs or high-durability cap sheets that melt flawlessly into multi-layered composites.

Primary industrial uses

  • Flooring manufacturing — primary wear layers for commercial PVC flooring
  • Textile alternatives — composite backing for synthetic leather
  • Advanced materials — multi-layer performance laminates

Technical parameters: High-width rolls (up to 1700 mm) with controlled surface energy and chemistry optimised for hot-melt or cold-adhesive lamination.

Custom chemical resistance & performance codes

Engineered additive packages, on request.

Specify any of these performance codes to tune a film to your mechanical and regulatory constraints.

FR-UL94

Flame retardant

Self-extinguishing additives meeting strict vertical and horizontal burn standards for electrical tapes, industrial curtains and building materials.

OR-FKM

Oil & hydrocarbon resistant

Engineered plasticizer networks that resist degradation, swelling or hardening on exposure to industrial oils, grease and automotive fluids.

UV-A10

Advanced UV stabilisers

Hindered amine light stabilisers (HALS) protecting agricultural and outdoor signage films from yellowing, chalking and polymer degradation.

SR-SHORE

Adjustable shore hardness

Precise plasticizer-to-resin balancing for custom flexibility from rigid 80 Shore A down to ultra-flexible 50 Shore A.

PH-FREE

Non-toxic safety

Bio-based / non-phthalate plasticizer compounding aligned to global children's-toy safety and medical-grade substrate regulations.
