Manufacturing & technology

Coated, converted and qualified — all under one roof.

DEON manufactures the complete tape in-house. Adhesive formulation, coating and converting on our own production lines — consistent chemistry, tolerances and documentation on every batch.

Capabilities

The Infrastructure Behind the Precision.

Enterprise capacity. Uninterrupted power. Absolute operational control. Our world-class manufacturing complex spans 60,000 sq. mtrs, powered by 500+ specialized professionals.

01

Raw Material Synthesizing & Calendaring Zone

The journey of a premium technical tape begins in our heavy chemical and substrate processing area, designed for high-throughput material creation from the ground up.

Adhesive Synthesis & Reactor Infrastructure

Automated, jacketed chemical reactors and mixing vessels compound our proprietary acrylic, rubber, hot-melt and silicone technologies. Computerized temperature management synthesizes polymers to precise molecular weights and uniform viscosities.

Toluene Recovery & Solvent Reclamation

Closed-loop toluene recovery plant captures and condenses solvent vapors through high-precision fractional distillation. Reclaimed high-purity toluene is reused immediately, minimizing waste and lowering VOC emissions.

Substrate Processing & Calendaring

2 high-capacity calendering units process raw polymers into high-precision, uniform PVC backings with customized branding, specialized printing and precise structural embossing built into the film.

Energy & Fuel Backbone

Specialized high-capacity steam boilers with secured, weatherproof coal storage ensure stable, independent thermal energy supply, protecting processes from external supply chain disruptions.

02

Controlled Coating & Thermal Curing

Advanced chemical formulas applied onto technical carrier films inside an environment completely isolated from external atmospheric changes and micro-contamination.

Positive-Pressure Clean Room

8 PVC coating lines, 2 BOPP coating lines, 1 hotmelt coating line, and 1 multi-coating line inside climate-controlled clean rooms. Multi-stage air handling filters particulate matter to micro-levels for zero-defect adhesive bonding.

Precision Thermal Curing

Advanced oil thermopack systems circulate specialized heat-transfer oils to maintain uniform, unwavering heat across the entire coating width for perfect chemical cross-linking.

Uninterrupted Power Protection

Synchronized network of heavy-duty industrial diesel generators with rapid-acting automated transfer panels carry the full electrical load instantly — zero seconds of downtime.

03

Plant Utilities, Converting & Material Handling

Once master jumbo rolls are coated and cured, they transition into high-speed converting bays through an integrated mechanical and logistical framework.

Heavy Material Handling

Fleet of industrial forklifts and manual hand pallet trolleys transport multi-ton master rolls across the 60,000 sq. mtr footprint with extreme care to prevent surface scratching or edge damage.

Centralized Utility Support

Industrial screw air compressors, high-capacity water chillers and cooling towers maintain sub-micron tolerances. Large-scale voltage stabilizers regulate power across the entire plant.

24/7 Dedicated Maintenance

Continuous-shift maintenance engineers perform real-time diagnostics and preventative calibrations across all chillers, cooling towers, boilers and compressors — zero unplanned downtime.

Converting, Finishing & Packing

High-speed logroll slitting into custom widths, finished as log rolls, jumbo rolls or retail units with printed cores and private-label packaging from our on-site artwork plant.

04

Closed-Loop Environmental Engineering

Heavy industrial capacity meets responsible environmental stewardship with sustainability integrated directly into daily utility operations.

4MW Solar Power

On-site photovoltaic arrays generate approximately 5.84 million kWh of clean electricity annually, offsetting carbon emissions and powering a significant portion of daily operations.

Zero-Discharge Water Treatment

100% of industrial wastewater undergoes multi-stage purification with advanced reverse osmosis and thermal evaporation. Zero liquid waste is discharged — purified water is recycled back into boilers and cooling towers.

Solid Waste & Material Circularity

All RM packaging is salvaged and repurposed. 100% of production scrap — paper matrix, cardboard, steel, aluminium, PVC — is collected, segregated and directed to specialized recycling plants.
