Accessibility statement

DEON is committed to making this website usable for as many people as possible.

Display options

Use the accessibility button in the header to adjust text size, contrast and motion. Preferences are saved on your device.

Standards

We aim to follow WCAG 2.1 AA guidance for colour contrast, keyboard operation and semantic structure.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier, contact info@deontapes.com and we will address it.
