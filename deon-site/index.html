Who we are

Manufacturer of pressure-sensitive adhesive tapes and soft PVC films.

Advanced testing & traceability

Extreme condition simulation

Every tape formulation is rigorously tested for peel adhesion, static shear, and long-term aging under extreme heat, freezing temperatures, high humidity, and heavy UV exposure.

Advanced testing & traceability

End-to-end digital traceability

Strict batch-tracking protocols. Every roll can be traced back to its specific raw material batch, adhesive mix, and production timestamp for absolute accountability.

Global market readiness

Our processes and raw materials align with strict global compliance standards, making our tapes fully eligible for export to the Americas, Europe, Asia, and beyond.
