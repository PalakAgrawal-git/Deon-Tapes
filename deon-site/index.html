How we make it

Coated, converted and qualified — all under one roof.

Every roll is coated, converted and qualified on our own lines — so the chemistry, the tolerances and the test report all come from one place.

01

Formulate & coat

Adhesive systems are formulated in-house and coated onto selected backing materials under controlled conditions.

02

Convert & slit

Products are slit, rewound and packaged according to specification.

03

Test & qualify

Production batches are tested and documented before release.
