Our journey

DEON's Journey

2016
Plant launched in Silvassa

First plant launched in Silvassa (20,000 sq. mtr). Capacity: ~16 Lakh sq. mtrs/month. Products: Electrical insulation, wire harness, floor marking tapes.

2017
BIS certification secured

Secured official BIS certification for core tape lines, clearing domestic tender filters and enabling participation in government and institutional procurement.

2018
ISO & UL certified

Achieved international ISO and UL certifications, establishing compliance credibility for MNC procurement and global export markets.

2019
Paper core plant commissioned

Backward integration: commissioned an in-house paper core plant, enabling precision winding control and eliminating third-party dependency for core supply.

2020
Masking tape launched

Expanded product portfolio with masking tape (painter’s tape), entering the commercial painting and surface protection market.

2021
Packaging corrugation plant added

Backward integration: added an in-house packaging corrugation plant, enabling self-sufficient packaging and zero delivery delays.

2022
₹100 Crore revenue milestone

Added 4 coating lines; capacity hit 50 Lakh sq. mtrs/month. Revenue crossed ₹100 Crore.

2023
Vadodara facility launched

Launched DEON Technology in Vadodara (+40,000 sq. mtr). Total area: 60,000 sq. mtrs; capacity: 2 Crore sq. mtrs/month.

2024
PVC calendering lines added

Backward integration: added 2 PVC calendering lines, manufacturing own PVC backing for electrical tape and eliminating import reliance.

2025
30+ specialty tape types launched

Launched BOPP, polyester, aluminium foil, polyimide, and 30+ specialty tape types from the Vadodara facility.

2026
₹330 Crore revenue & 500+ employees

Active 60,000 sq. mtr infrastructure across two plants. Revenue: ₹330 Crore | Net Worth: ₹250 Crore. 500+ employees.

2030
Next-gen expansion & IPO

Next-gen plant expansion planned. IPO public stock listing. Target revenue: ₹1,000 Crore.

The DEON Factory Advantage

Vertical Integration: Total Quality Control & Rapid Delivery

At our state-of-the-art DEON factory, we do not just assemble tapes — we manufacture them from the ground up. Every single step of the development and production process is handled 100% in-house, eliminating third-party dependencies to maintain strict quality standards and guarantee exceptionally fast delivery.

Testing, Traceability & Compliance

Performance verified. Globally compliant.

01

Extreme condition simulation

Every tape formulation is rigorously tested for peel adhesion, static shear and long-term ageing under extreme heat, freezing temperatures, high humidity and heavy UV exposure.

02

Digital traceability

Strict batch-tracking protocols — every roll traces back to its specific raw material batch, adhesive mix and production timestamp for absolute accountability.

03

Global market readiness

Manufacturing processes and raw materials align with strict global compliance standards, making our tapes fully eligible for export to the Americas, Europe, Asia and beyond.

04

Eco-conscious safety

In-house PVC film calendaring and chemical adhesive synthesis focus on eliminating toxic hazards, ensuring safe usage in commercial and sensitive industrial environments.
