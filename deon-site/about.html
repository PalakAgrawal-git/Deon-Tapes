About DEON

Manufacturing owned from formulation to finished roll.

DEON manufactures the complete tape in-house — adhesive formulation, coating and converting on its own production lines. The same formulation, tolerances and documentation apply to every production batch.

Who we are

An Indian manufacturer of industrial tape and soft PVC film.

DEON manufactures pressure-sensitive adhesive tapes and soft PVC films for customers across India and international markets — adhesive formulation, coating, converting, quality control and product development, all controlled in-house.
