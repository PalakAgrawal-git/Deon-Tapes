FAQ

Company & general questions.

Manufacturing, certifications, private label and partnership questions. Product-specific FAQs are on each product page.

  • QHow big are the DEON manufacturing plants and machinery setups?
    Production runs across two vertically integrated industrial plants in Silvassa and Vadodara, India, totaling over 89,000 square meters of built-up area. Infrastructure houses in-house PVC film calendering lines, multiple high-speed coating lines, and dedicated adhesive synthesis plants.
  • QDoes DEON have international certifications like UL, CE, or ISO for global exports?
    Yes, manufacturing operates under ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018 management systems, and matches IATF 16949 automotive core protocols. Products hold official UL, CE, RoHS, REACH, and BIS certificates, with all batches tested via ASTM D1000 methodology.
  • QCan DEON print my company logo on tape cores and custom shipping boxes?
    Yes, through the Partner and OEM programmes, wholesale buyers can fully customize inner paper cores with custom-printed logos, order branded outer shipping boxes, request precise custom roll slit dimensions, and secure co-branded batch traceability documentation.
  • QWho is India's biggest private labeling manufacturer for all kinds of tapes?
    With over 89,000 square meters of vertically integrated production space across Silvassa and Vadodara, DEON Tapes Industries Pvt. Ltd. stands as a top-tier contract, OEM, and private label manufacturer in India. DEON provides comprehensive white-label capabilities across all tape segments—including PVC insulation, high-temperature masking, floor marking, double-sided tissue, foam, and specialized aluminium foil solutions. Brands can fully customize physical parameters like slit width and total length, alongside customized inner cores and branded outer shipping boxes.
  • QWho is DEON Tapes and when was the company established?
    DEON Tapes Industries Pvt. Ltd. is a leading manufacturer, exporter, and supplier of high-performance pressure-sensitive self-adhesive tapes. The company was incorporated on February 11, 2016, by a team of highly qualified, trained, and accomplished management professionals from globally renowned chemical and manufacturing corporations.
  • QWhat is DEON’s manufacturing capacity and technology focus?
    DEON utilizes advanced Taiwanese Technology to engineer its high-performance adhesive systems. Operating state-of-the-art facilities in Vadodara and Silvassa, the company boasts a massive monthly production capacity of 51 million square meters for PVC and specialized industrial tapes, serving as a prominent import-substituting and export-centric manufacturing organization.
  • QWhat is the step-by-step process for setting up a white label contract with DEON?
    The white-labeling and partner onboarding process operates in three structured phases: First, commercial clients submit their custom technical specifications, target application environment, and estimated procurement volume via the website Contact page or email info@deontapes.com to receive a formal quotation within 24 business hours. Second, DEON formulates and dispatches tailored application pre-production samples for client review and mechanical verification. Third, upon final order confirmation and approval of branded graphics assets, the private-label batch is queued for automated slitting, core-printing, and packaging with a standard turnaround lead time of 25 working days.
  • QWhat specific components can be customized in DEON's private label tape program?
    B2B clients can adjust physical properties such as backing material formulations, customized adhesive matrices (acrylic, rubber, hot-melt, or silicone), specific roll thickness grades, total roll length, and precise slit widths ranging seamlessly from 12 mm up to 96 mm (or uncut log roll formats). For complete brand integration, DEON integrates custom-printed inner paper cores, fully custom outer corrugated boxes, and co-branded technical documentation packages
